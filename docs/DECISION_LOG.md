**Project Name:** ShareTextQR

**Document Version:** 1.0.0

**Status:** 🔒 ACTIVE

# Purpose

This document records all major product, UI, UX, SEO and architectural decisions made during the ShareTextQR project.

It provides a historical record of why decisions were made and ensures future development remains consistent with the approved Product Requirements Document (PRD).

This document complements `MASTER_SPECIFICATION.md` and must be updated whenever a significant product decision is approved.

---

# Decision Record Format

Every decision should follow this format.

---

## Decision ID

Unique identifier.

Example:

DEC-001

---

## Date

Date of approval.

Example:

2026-07-25

---

## Category

Examples

• Product

• UI

• UX

• SEO

• Architecture

• Repository

• Performance

• Accessibility

• Development

---

## Decision

Brief summary of the approved decision.

---

## Reason

Explain why the decision was made.

---

## Impact

Describe the areas affected.

Examples

• Homepage

• Tool Pages

• SEO

• Repository

• Components

---

## Related PRD Chapter

Reference the relevant chapter(s) from `MASTER_SPECIFICATION.md`.
